Lantern Tales is a TikTok channel that retells myths, folktales and beliefs from around the world in short animated episodes. The channel is made for viewers aged 18 and over.

Every video is AI-generated and is labelled as AI-generated content when it is posted on TikTok. Stories are retellings of public-domain myths and folklore.

About Toon Studio Uploader

Toon Studio Uploader is the TikTok developer app behind this channel. It is a private, internal tool used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

Here is what it does:

  1. The channel owner's own production system creates a new episode.
  2. Toon Studio Uploader sends the finished video file to the owner's TikTok inbox, using TikTok Login Kit and the video.upload permission only.
  3. The owner opens the TikTok app, reviews the draft, turns on the AI-generated content label, and decides whether to post it.

The tool never posts automatically. Nothing is published until the owner posts it by hand in the TikTok app.
